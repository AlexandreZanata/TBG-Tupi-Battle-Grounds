# Field-news banners

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using the existing medieval RPG
card illustrations as a palette/composition reference. No external images,
lettering or baked-in UI frames. Original generated PNGs are retained in the
ignored task workspace; project consumers use these optimized raster assets:

- ../../crates/tbg-client/assets/lobby/news/patch-notes.webp
- ../../crates/tbg-client/assets/lobby/news/forum.webp
- ../../crates/tbg-client/assets/lobby/news/game-news.webp

All are 1200x400 WebP, quality 84. UI draws its own gold frame and localized
text. The forum illustration represents planned community work, not a claim
that a forum service has shipped. No SVG conversion was performed.

## Final prompt set

### Patch notes

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one premium raster illustration for the PATCH NOTES news banner of Tupi Battle Grounds, a medieval fantasy strategy game. Panoramic composition 1536 x 512, full bleed, no text, no letters, no typography, no logo, no watermark, no UI border. A beautifully illuminated parchment and an elegant golden balance scale, a steel sword and a quill resting on a dark navy oak war-room desk, a small red wax seal, restrained blue and crimson heraldic cloth behind them. Hand-painted high-end game key art, richly modeled gold and brushed steel, the visual language of detailed medieval RPG card illustrations. Dark navy and charcoal shadows, warm antique gold highlights, controlled red and blue accents, dramatic warm light with crisp focal detail. Keep the important objects in the central half so the image crops gracefully into a wide 3:1 lobby news slot. One cohesive professional illustration, no collage.

### Forum

Use case: stylized-concept. One premium panoramic raster illustration for the COMMUNITY FORUM banner of Tupi Battle Grounds, cohesive with high-end medieval RPG card art. Full bleed wide 3:1 composition, no text, no readable writing, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no UI frame. A medieval war council's round oak table in a noble stone hall, three empty chairs around the table, an open blank parchment, a few rolled scrolls and elegant quills, small blue and red heraldic pennants gathered peacefully around a golden central lantern. The clear focal motif is the table that invites discussion and community, welcoming rather than threatening. Rich dark navy/charcoal, antique gold metalwork, tasteful blue and crimson cloth. Painterly detailed fantasy game key art with refined materials and warm cinematic light, sharp professional finish, generous dark breathing room at edges, main scene readable at a small lobby banner size. Keep important objects in center half for graceful wide cropping. Not a collage, no speech bubble UI.

### Game news

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one premium panoramic 3:1 raster illustration for the GAME NEWS banner of Tupi Battle Grounds. Cohesive medieval fantasy RPG key-art style, full bleed, no letters, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no UI border. A polished golden herald's trumpet and a rolled parchment scroll with a simple wax seal in the foreground on a dark stone parapet, behind them a beautiful verdant battlefield between two distant imposing medieval strongholds, one with royal blue pennants and one with crimson pennants, dawn light illuminating the towers, lush pines and a hint of water. The trumpet and scroll symbolize announcements; show a calm epic vista, no active attack or gore. Antique gold metal, dark navy and charcoal shadows, restrained red and blue accents, painterly rich materials with sharp professional fantasy-game detail. Warm cinematic lighting and harmonious contrast, important subjects in the center half for graceful crop into a small wide lobby panel. Same visual quality as a premium illustrated card set. One cohesive scene, not a collage.
