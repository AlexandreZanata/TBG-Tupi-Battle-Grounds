## Game assets (active project)

No third-party art, audio, or fonts are bundled. Figurines, arena, and
effects are generated by code (`crates/tbg-client/src/art/`, `arena/`,
`vfx/`); the 11 sound effects in `crates/tbg-client/assets/audio/` are
synthesized by `crates/tbg-client/examples/make_sfx.rs`. Both are original
work of this project and fall under the project's license. Text uses
Bevy's built-in default font (Fira Mono, SIL Open Font License 1.1, shipped
inside the `bevy` crate).
