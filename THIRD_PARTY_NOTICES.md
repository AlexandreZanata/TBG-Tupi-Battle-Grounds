## Native authentication dependencies (P68-A)

The Linux candidate uses keyring-core 1.0.1 and
`dbus-secret-service-keyring-store` 1.0.1 with Rust crypto and vendored D-Bus.
The Windows provider is `windows-native-keyring-store` 1.1.0. These crates,
`dbus-secret-service` 4.1.0, `dbus` 0.9.12, `libdbus-sys` 0.2.7 and `ureq`
3.5.0 are dual MIT/Apache-2.0 licensed. Exact upstream license texts are in
`deploy/native/licenses/`, copied to every native package's `licenses/`.
The vendored libdbus library is offered under AFL-2.1 or GPL-2.0-or-later;
its original combined COPYING is included as `licenses/libdbus-COPYING`.
Only the client library is linked; D-Bus tools/services are not installed by
the game. No new external authentication service is introduced.
