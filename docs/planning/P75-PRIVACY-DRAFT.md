# P75 privacy notice draft — owner review required

This is an activation draft, not a published privacy policy or compliance claim. Existing public policy remains in place until owner approval.

P75 collects a normalized private email and preferred email language for account verification and recovery. The email is never public; the account panel displays a masked address. A verified mailbox can replace the AccountKey on the same account. Losing both the key and mailbox cannot be solved through the game. Operator identities are separate from player accounts.

Match chat is plain text between the two players. The server stores at most 200 masked entries/32 KiB in a social record for seven days. A report copies relevant server evidence; closed reports are retained for 180 days. Reports and reporter identity are visible to authorized operators, never the reported player. Moderation/audit history is retained. No report count automatically punishes an account.

Uploaded PNG/GIF/static-WebP images are normalized; originals are discarded. Only operator-approved normalized sheets become public. Rejected or withdrawn sheets are removed after 30 days while moderation metadata remains. Mail intent rows are retained for 30 days after completion; raw keys/codes/session tokens are not persisted. SMTP and the mailbox provider handle delivery under the owner's chosen provider terms.

Before activation, the owner must publish accurate contact details, decide a request/account-deletion process and the treatment of retained competitive/moderation records, review provider processing and backup retention, and approve the final EN/pt-BR notice. No account-deletion route is implemented in P75.
