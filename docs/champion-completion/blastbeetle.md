**Status: Proposed.** Owner-authorized champion completion; numbers are initial local-preview hypotheses, not measured balance.

Display names: **Blastbeetle** / **Besouro-Estalo**. Insects / DPS.

Default skin: Volatile Amber / Âmbar Volátil.

## Identity and crowd space

Small lean click-beetle with arched thorax and glowing faceted abdomen, two short claws and four small feet; no Tank shell.

Abdomen is mostly longitudinal; no wide wing opening, explosion visuals never push crowd members.

Use the shared vertex-color material, cached per-joint meshes and existing rig slots. Sample actual posed bounds for all five forms; retain one-dimensional authoritative anchors and existing crowd limits. No visual overlap waiver or simulation-body resizing.

## Props, shots and motion

### Weapon

Short striking foreclaws and the organic abdomen; no metal bomb or modern fuse.

### Projectile

None; one bounded near-death amber pop with flat shard shapes, no recursive explosions.

### Idle

Small thorax spring compression and contained abdomen light pulses.

### Walk

Fast springy four-foot scuttle; shell lids remain close to abdomen.

### Attack

Short paired-claw jab presented as one basic hit, followed by a visible spring recoil.

### Special

Combat death resolves one capped local damage burst; sale or replacement never triggers it.

### Victory

Two tiny grounded clicks, abdomen sway and shell-lid bow; no victory explosion.

### Death

Legs compress, shell opens a little and the single server-authorized pop plays before the body settles.
