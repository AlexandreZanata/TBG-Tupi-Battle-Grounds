# Cinder Maul / Marreta de Brasa

Proposed OG-01; Ogres, Dps.

stocky brick-red ogre with low coal-black brow, two short square-headed iron forge hammers held vertically near ribs, asymmetric leather apron and small glowing coals in a rear basket, no guns.

Server ability: Every third completed hit sends a compact cinder sweep into nearby enemies.

Evolution A: one tall double-faced hammer, pale forged plates and upright coal crown. Evolution B: twin charcoal hammers with orange cracks, oxblood apron and iron collar. Every tier grows by the standard 8/16 percent, changes a large material region and adds equipment.

Victory: Alternates hammer lifts to a forge rhythm. Spawn, hit and death use the authored per-kind life choices in ogres/motion.rs. Equipment stays compact; paired hand targets use the shared IK. Foot rings identify teams; body materials and meshes are identical for both seats.

Projectile and impact: the Ogre projectile recipe selects a faceted pebble, earth clod, bone stitch or steel/brass rivet; melee kits use per-kind chips, arcs or cinders. Existing server hit/release timing remains authoritative.

Forms:

- A1: Coalbreaker / Quebra-Carvão.
- A2: Ember Anvil / Bigorna Incandescente.
- B1: Twin Brands / Marcas Gêmeas.
- B2: Furnace Fists / Punhos da Fornalha.
