# Rivet Ballista / Balista de Rebites

Proposed OG-01; Machines, Ranged.

compact upright medieval clockwork ballista automaton with two long bent iron legs and broad brass feet, faceted ivory wedge head, narrow forward bolt rail gripped by jointed arms, vertical rivet magazine rising behind head, warm brass chest and dark iron pelvis; no ogre skin, wheels, cannon or drill.

Server ability: Every third rivet pierces one target behind; precise replacement for Machines ranged slot.
