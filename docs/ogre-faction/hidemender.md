# Hide Mender / Remendeiro de Couro

Proposed OG-01; Ogres, Support.

warm gray-green ogre medic with round spectacles, patchwork tan leather shoulder cape, large carved bone sewing needle in right hand, thick amber thread spool carried vertically on back, a stitched leather satchel at left hip; no cooking equipment, no wings.

Server ability: Stitches apply several small healing pulses; feet stable for rear allies.

Evolution A: cream stitched mantle and ivory needle with small brass loop. Evolution B: dark patchwork cape, larger amber spool with pale thread halo. Every tier grows by the standard 8/16 percent, changes a large material region and adds equipment.

Victory: Raises the needle and twirls the threaded wrist. Spawn, hit and death use the authored per-kind life choices in ogres/motion.rs. Equipment stays compact; paired hand targets use the shared IK. Foot rings identify teams; body materials and meshes are identical for both seats.

Projectile and impact: the Ogre projectile recipe selects a faceted pebble, earth clod, bone stitch or steel/brass rivet; melee kits use per-kind chips, arcs or cinders. Existing server hit/release timing remains authoritative.

Forms:

- A2: Leather Surgeon / Cirurgião do Couro.
- B1: Spirit Spool / Carretel Espiritual.
