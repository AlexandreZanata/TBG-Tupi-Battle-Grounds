# Flint Runner / Corredor de Sílex

Proposed OG-01; Ogres, Dps.

tall wiry sand-colored ogre with long wrapped legs, forward neck and two small chipped obsidian cleavers held close to hips, swept-back white hair crest, rust-orange short mantle, no shield or firearm.

Server ability: Walking charges the next hit; fast flanking silhouette without teleportation.

Evolution A: long pale flint blade and white leather shoulder mantle. Evolution B: paired black obsidian cleavers with amber mineral edges and rust wraps. Every tier grows by the standard 8/16 percent, changes a large material region and adds equipment.

Victory: Makes short heel hops with the right cleaver raised. Spawn, hit and death use the authored per-kind life choices in ogres/motion.rs. Equipment stays compact; paired hand targets use the shared IK. Foot rings identify teams; body materials and meshes are identical for both seats.

Projectile and impact: the Ogre projectile recipe selects a faceted pebble, earth clod, bone stitch or steel/brass rivet; melee kits use per-kind chips, arcs or cinders. Existing server hit/release timing remains authoritative.
