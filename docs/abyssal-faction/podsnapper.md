# Pod Snapper / Estala-Vagens

Proposed AB-01; Plants / Ranged. Private preview only.

## Identity and costume

small upright living wood creature with a broad split seed-pod launcher enclosing its right forearm, three large ivory seeds visible inside the green jaws of the pod, asymmetrical curled leaf crown, squat root feet and ochre bark chest, no face mask or mushrooms. Base costume: Seedkeeper / Guardião das Sementes.
Intrinsic colors never change with seat; only the foot ring supplies ownership.

## Forms

Base retains the readable species/weapon. A emphasizes pale fitted shell
protection and precise equipment; B uses sea-plum asymmetry and extra fins.
Tier-one scale is 1.08 and tier-two 1.16. Pod Snapper uses bark/leaf/sap instead.

- A1: Ironpod / Vagem de Ferro.
- A2: Royal Seeder / Sementeira Real.
- B1: Split Husk / Casca Estalada.
- B2: Harvest Burst / Estouro da Colheita.

## Combat, weapon and effects

Every third completed hit plants one bounded non-stacking seed mark consumed by the next allied melee strike.

Split living forearm pod with three visible seeds and animated jaws. Ivory seed with green pod tip; its shared mark has bounded public duration. All outcomes and special centers are server-owned.
Shared primitive bounds, healing reduction and control/bleed replacement apply.

## Motion

Grounded two-bone walk follows authoritative travel. Idle has restrained
head/chest movement; attack preparation and release follow the shared combat
event timing with per-kind grip and torso accents. Spawn, Evolution, hit and
death use the native rig with the kit's weight/weapon posture.
Victory: Bobs its leaf crown and lifts the seed pod.

## Clearance and validation

Compact carried equipment and actual sampled mesh envelopes reserve cosmetic
space for arms, jaws, fins and held weapons. No gameplay collision or range
is derived from art. Existing lane-offset caps still apply in saturated crowds.

Concept: built-in imagegen five-form reference, separate from engine output.
Native captures, source/hash manifests and scoped evidence are recorded by
[the phase report](../phases/ABYSSALS-OGRE-REFINEMENT.md). Windows/game-web
visual comparison and competitive acceptance remain pending until observed.
