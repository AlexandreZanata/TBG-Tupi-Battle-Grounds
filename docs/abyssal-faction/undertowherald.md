# Undertow Herald / Arauto da Ressaca

Proposed AB-01; Abyssals / Control. Private preview only.

## Identity and costume

broad-necked amphibious herald with three small chin fins, large spiral conch horn held near chest in both hands, flat scalloped shoulder mantle, ivory head stripe and deep green skin. Base costume: Ancient Conch / Concha Antiga.
Intrinsic colors never change with seat; only the foot ring supplies ownership.

## Forms

Base retains the readable species/weapon. A emphasizes pale fitted shell
protection and precise equipment; B uses sea-plum asymmetry and extra fins.
Tier-one scale is 1.08 and tier-two 1.16. Pod Snapper uses bark/leaf/sap instead.

- A1: Resonant Conch / Concha Ressonante.
- A2: Undertow Voice / Voz da Ressaca.
- B1: Tide Horn / Trompa da Maré.
- B2: Maelstrom Call / Chamado do Maelstrom.

## Combat, weapon and effects

Periodic announced conch pulses slow a bounded group near the primary target.

Flared spiral conch held at chest height; branched mouth/ribs change with Evolution. Spiral shell pulse; delayed Slow has a separate authoritative cue. All outcomes and special centers are server-owned.
Shared primitive bounds, healing reduction and control/bleed replacement apply.
