# Foam Weaver / Tecelã de Espuma

Proposed AB-01; Abyssals / Support. Private preview only.

## Identity and costume

graceful amphibious healer with two lateral soft head fins, an open half-shell chalice held in both hands with a small suspended pearl, overlapping white nacre skirt panels, plum shoulder scarf and sea-green skin. Base costume: Foam Mantle / Manto de Espuma.
Intrinsic colors never change with seat; only the foot ring supplies ownership.

## Forms

Base retains the readable species/weapon. A emphasizes pale fitted shell
protection and precise equipment; B uses sea-plum asymmetry and extra fins.
Tier-one scale is 1.08 and tier-two 1.16. Pod Snapper uses bark/leaf/sap instead.

- A1: Foam Chalice / Taça de Espuma.
- A2: Nacre Spring / Fonte de Nácar.
- B1: Tide Threads / Fios da Maré.
- B2: Surf Mantle / Manto da Arrebentação.

## Combat, weapon and effects

Healing leaves a short non-stacking echo consumed by the next nonlethal health hit.

Half-shell chalice, open fins and one suspended pearl. Small pearlescent foam cluster; the healing echo remains visible on the patient. All outcomes and special centers are server-owned.
Shared primitive bounds, healing reduction and control/bleed replacement apply.

## Motion

Grounded two-bone walk follows authoritative travel. Idle has restrained
head/chest movement; attack preparation and release follow the shared combat
event timing with per-kind grip and torso accents. Spawn, Evolution, hit and
death use the native rig with the kit's weight/weapon posture.
Victory: Presents the pearl with both arms and turns the upper body gently.

space for arms, jaws, fins and held weapons. No gameplay collision or range
