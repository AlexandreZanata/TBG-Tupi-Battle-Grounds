# Trench Lamplighter / Faroleiro da Fossa

Proposed AB-01; Abyssals / Control. Private preview only.

## Identity and costume

very tall thin angler-faced lamplighter with short bent fishing-lure head fin, an amber glass lantern inside a cage of ivory shell ribs on a compact staff, dark indigo split robe and pale green hands. Base costume: Last Beacon / Último Farol.
Intrinsic colors never change with seat; only the foot ring supplies ownership.

## Forms

Base retains the readable species/weapon. A emphasizes pale fitted shell
protection and precise equipment; B uses sea-plum asymmetry and extra fins.
Tier-one scale is 1.08 and tier-two 1.16. Pod Snapper uses bark/leaf/sap instead.

- A1: Amber Lantern / Lanterna de Âmbar.
- A2: Deep Beacon / Farol das Profundezas.
- B1: Saltglass / Vidro Salino.
- B2: Abyss Eye / Olho do Abismo.

## Combat, weapon and effects

A delayed light pulse strikes a locked visible ground point; enemies can walk out.

Amber lantern enclosed by shell ribs on a short staff; living lure on the head. Amber faceted shard for basics; the special marks a fixed ground center before resolving. All outcomes and special centers are server-owned.
Shared primitive bounds, healing reduction and control/bleed replacement apply.

## Motion

Grounded two-bone walk follows authoritative travel. Idle has restrained
head/chest movement; attack preparation and release follow the shared combat
event timing with per-kind grip and torso accents. Spawn, Evolution, hit and
death use the native rig with the kit's weight/weapon posture.
Victory: Raises the lantern and follows it with a lifted head.
