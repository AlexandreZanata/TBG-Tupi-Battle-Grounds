# Abyss Harpooner / Arpoadora do Abismo

Proposed AB-01; Abyssals / Ranged. Private preview only.

## Identity and costume

upright eel-faced huntress with long narrow jaw, short back fin, compact hand-held wooden harpoon launcher with a clearly visible long ivory barbed bolt, cream chest straps and dark sea-plum coat. Base costume: Trench Hunt / Caçada das Fossas.
Intrinsic colors never change with seat; only the foot ring supplies ownership.

## Forms

Base retains the readable species/weapon. A emphasizes pale fitted shell
protection and precise equipment; B uses sea-plum asymmetry and extra fins.
Tier-one scale is 1.08 and tier-two 1.16. Pod Snapper uses bark/leaf/sap instead.

- A1: Nacre Harpoon / Arpão de Nácar.
- A2: Trench Sight / Mira das Fossas.
- B1: Barbed Spear / Lança Farpada.
- B2: Abyss Hunt / Caçada Abissal.

## Combat, weapon and effects

Barbed bolts apply one non-stacking bleed family.

Wood-and-nacre hand-held launcher with barbed bolt and branch-B back bolts. Slender barbed ivory bolt with a wooden shaft. All outcomes and special centers are server-owned.
Shared primitive bounds, healing reduction and control/bleed replacement apply.

## Motion

Grounded two-bone walk follows authoritative travel. Idle has restrained
head/chest movement; attack preparation and release follow the shared combat
event timing with per-kind grip and torso accents. Spawn, Evolution, hit and
death use the native rig with the kit's weight/weapon posture.
Victory: Presents the launcher and tilts the shoulders.

## Clearance and validation

Compact carried equipment and actual sampled mesh envelopes reserve cosmetic
space for arms, jaws, fins and held weapons. No gameplay collision or range
is derived from art. Existing lane-offset caps still apply in saturated crowds.
