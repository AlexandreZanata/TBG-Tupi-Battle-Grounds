# P75 account recovery and match social — candidate

**Status:** Draft candidate; not deployed or enabled. Ruleset 15 / `cc459351581b7763` is unchanged. Wire 39 and additive schema 12 require attended activation.

## Player changes

New accounts require an email. Verify it with a code and use Lost your key to receive a new key on the same account; history, Rating, Arcanas, Stronghold and owned data remain. The old key and sessions are revoked. Existing accounts keep playing and may add an email using their current key.

Online matches gain private two-seat chat, mute, three emoji slots and a standard emoji set. Custom PNG/GIF/WebP uploads require email verification and operator review of every normalized frame. Reports use evidence captured by the server. Suspensions show their reason and end time. Chat and uploads start disabled until the Owner enables them.

EN/pt-BR localized copy is included in the patch catalog. Linux is the reference; real Linux/Windows/web correspondence evidence and owner look/fun sign-off remain pending. No combat rule, balance value, simulation source or RulesetVersion changes.
