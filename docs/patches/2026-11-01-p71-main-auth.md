Protocol: 31. Schema: 10. Ruleset: 6 / 097385fee9a10b0f.

This combines main's protected authentication, absolute-lifetime rotating refresh,
secure native persistence and authenticated reconnect with the owner's P70/P71
character, Stronghold and eight-Arena branch. The protocol-30 lines are incompatible
with the integrated client/server. Existing authentication lifetimes do not change.

Compared with main, P71 adds independent Tick troop pistol attacks (damage 12,
range 3000, period 30 ticks; B1 period 24, B2 damage 18), character/form/weapon
readability fixes, persistent Evolution focus below the timer, and cosmetic
Stronghold degradation. These gameplay values are Proposed and have no claimed
D-33 acceptance. Earlier P70/P71 candidate limits remain in their source reports.

The original five Arena modifiers remain unchanged. Celestial adds 8% movement;
Clockwork adds 8% attack speed and reduces movement by 5%; Desert reduces base
Energy regeneration by 8%. All three are symmetric, Proposed playtest values;
Interest combines with the signed Arena offset before fractional accumulation.
Local reference images are excluded from source control and deployment transfer.
